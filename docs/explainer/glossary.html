Glossary

Every term of the documentation that a newcomer may not know, from thread and cache to Holm and systemd scope. Each has a one-sentence definition and a paragraph with an example from this project. You can list the terms by the page that explains them or from A to Z.

On every page, a word with a dotted underline is one of these: hover or tap it and the plain sentence appears; the link in that box brings you here, to the example. Nothing on the pages assumes you know a word before it has been explained, so if one is missing, it is a bug.
