Glossary

Every word the guide uses that a newcomer might not know, from thread and cache to Holm and systemd scope. Each one gets a plain sentence anyone can read, then a short paragraph with an example from this project and the file or decision it comes from.

In one sentence: a dictionary of the project in two layers per word, the plain sentence and the example, grouped by the page that explains the word, or from A to Z.

On every page, a word with a dotted underline is one of these: hover or tap it and the plain sentence appears; the link in that box brings you here, to the example. Nothing on the pages assumes you know a word before it has been explained, so if one is missing, it is a bug.
